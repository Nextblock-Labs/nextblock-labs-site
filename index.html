How it works
01
Discovery — find where the time goes
One conversation. We'll ask blunt questions about your day-to-day and find the three or four things that are eating your team's time. Most clients are surprised how obvious the answer is once someone looks.
02
Scope — agree what gets built
Fixed deliverable, fixed price, documented before anything starts. You know exactly what you're getting and exactly what it costs. No surprises mid-build.
03
Build — you see it as it happens
Not a black box. We build in plain sight, show you working versions as they're ready, and adjust if something isn't right. Typically weeks, not months.
04
Handover — it's yours
Full handover. You own the system, the code, the documentation. Bug fixes included for 30 days after delivery; after that, it runs on its own — or we can help on a per-job basis if you need it. No retainer, no lock-in.
